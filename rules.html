Book Club Rules

  1. 1

    We spin the wheel to choose our weekly book.

    Each week we will select our new book by spinning the wheel. We obey the wheel. All hale the wheel.

  2. 2

    We read one book a week.

    Unless a book is over 700 pages, then we may split it across 2 weeks. That is for everyone’s benefit. NOT JUST AUNTIE MARI, OKAY?!?!

  3. 3

    We keep notes as we read.

    As we read, we will each keep notes in our booklog/book journal to help with our discussions.

  4. 4

    When it’s part of a series, we only read the first book.

    We all agree to read the first book. IF the whole group decides they want to keep going, then we will finish the series before selecting a new book.

  5. 5

    We always stay kind and respectful.

    We don’t have to all agree or have the same opinions. But we do have to be kind about all disagreements.

  6. 6

    We make an effort to read the whole book.

    We all agree to make a best effort to finish every book, even we don’t like it. It’s no fun to discuss a book if not everyone finished it.
