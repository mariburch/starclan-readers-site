Star in the Forest

Laura Resau

Standalone

When 11-year-old Zitlally's father is arrested and deported to Mexico, her family attempts to bring him back, only to learn he has been kidnapped for ransom. Seeking comfort, Zitlally rescues a stray white dog with a star-shaped patch over its eye, and becomes convinced that keeping Star safe will help her father find his way back home.
