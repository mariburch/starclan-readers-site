The Void Dragons

Scott Reintgen

The Dragonships Series, Book 3 of 3

A year after Neptune’s threat, Lunar Jones and the Dread Knights are training new riders and preparing for the Solar Summit, a gathering of planetary dragons held only once every 500 years. Then Lunar finds an abandoned ship drifting between Earth and Mars, its crew gone, and the clues point to nine creatures coming for all nine planets. Something even bigger is behind them: a black hole.
