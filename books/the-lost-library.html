The Lost Library

Rebecca Stead & Wendy Mass

Standalone

When a mysterious free little library guarded by a large orange cat appears in town, 11-year-old Evan takes two books that spark a quest to solve a long-ago library fire. Told through the alternating perspectives of Evan, Mortimer the cat, and a ghost librarian named Al, the three stories piece together the town's lost history.
